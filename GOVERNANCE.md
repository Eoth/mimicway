# Governance

Mimicway has one maintainer, who decides. This page says who holds which role, what each role does, and how decisions are made and contested.

## Roles

| Role | Who | Responsibilities |
|---|---|---|
| Maintainer | [@Eoth](https://github.com/Eoth) | Sets the direction and keeps [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md); reviews, approves and merges changes; cuts releases ([CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md#releasing)); answers bug reports and feature requests; handles vulnerability reports ([SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)) and code of conduct reports ([CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)); administers the repository, its settings and its secrets. |
| Contributor | Anyone who opens an issue or a pull request | Follows [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) and the [code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). |

## How decisions are made

- The maintainer makes the final decision on every change, release and roadmap item.
- A proposal starts as an issue or as a comment on a roadmap item, before any code, when it adds a feature or bends one of the promises at the top of ROADMAP.md ("What Mimicway stays"). The decision and its reason are written there, or in ROADMAP.md when it shapes later work.
- A pull request is merged when it keeps those promises, follows CONTRIBUTING.md, passes the CI (`CI passed`) and has the maintainer's approval.
- A disagreement is argued in the issue or pull request where it started; the maintainer decides and says why. Anyone may fork the project under its [license](LICENSE).

## Changes to this page

When regular contributors appear, the maintainer may invite some of them to share the maintainer role; this page then lists them and says how they decide together. Any change to it goes through a pull request like any other.
